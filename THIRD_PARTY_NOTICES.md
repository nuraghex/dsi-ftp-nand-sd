## Fonts

The 8x8 glyphs in `source/font_data.c` are generated by `tools/mkfont.py` from:

| Font | Used for | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| [unscii-8](https://github.com/viznut/unscii) by viznut (`tools/fonts/unscii-8.hex`) | Latin, Greek, Cyrillic, symbols | Public domain (CC0) |
| [Fusion Pixel Font](https://github.com/TakWolf/fusion-pixel-font) 8px, release 2026.10.25, by TakWolf | Kana and CJK ideographs | SIL Open Font License 1.1 |

Fusion Pixel Font is assembled from several open pixel fonts (Ark Pixel, Misaki, MisekiBitmap, BoutiqueBitmap7x7, Galmuri). Its licence and the licences of these components are in `licenses/fusion-pixel-font/` and must be included in binary releases too. The glyphs are embedded in the program as bitmap data; they are not distributed as a font under the Fusion Pixel name.
